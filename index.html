Host cheat sheet

Easy:
1. "Clear cart" does nothing.
2. "Track your order" (footer) goes to a 404 page.

Medium:
3. Total counts the Cake slice once, however many are added (add 2+).
4. The "Cart: N items" badge counts different products, not quantities (add 2 croissants: it says 1).

Tricky:
5. "Apply" with an empty coupon box shows "undefined% off applied" and the total becomes ₹NaN.
6. "save10" in lowercase is rejected, although the box says any case works. "SAVE10" works.
